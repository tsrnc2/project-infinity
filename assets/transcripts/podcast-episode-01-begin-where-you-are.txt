Begin Where You Are

Elder Rowan: Welcome to The Turning Life. I am Elder Rowan, and today we begin where we are.

Maya Vale: And I am Maya Vale. I will walk beside the teaching, asking the questions a first circle might ask.

Elder Rowan: The first practice is witness. Witness is not confession for shame. Witness is truthful sight.

Maya Vale: So the question is not, how do I look holy today? The question is, what is actually true?

Elder Rowan: Yes. Name the current state without performance, without denial, and without cruelty. The will cannot transform what the mind refuses to see.

Maya Vale: Today I can write three lines: what is real, what I have avoided, and what I will practice before night.

Elder Rowan: If the truth is small, honor it. If the truth is painful, hold it gently. If the truth reveals harm, move toward repair.

Maya Vale: This also protects people in recovery. A craving, a relapse, or a hidden pattern is not healed by pretending. It is met by witness, support, and one sober next step.

Elder Rowan: No circle should claim to replace medical care, counseling, peer support, or emergency help. The circle offers witness and practice, not captivity.

Maya Vale: I begin where I am. I do not need to pretend I am finished.

Elder Rowan: Breathe once. Name one truth. Choose one action. This is the gate of transformation.

Maya Vale: May I tell the truth I can bear, then grow strong enough to tell more.

Elder Rowan: May your witness become refinement, and may your refinement become service. The turning begins now.
