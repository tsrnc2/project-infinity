The Turning Life

Dialogues with Father Rowan, an elder priest with a slow British-Boston cadence, and adult partners Maya Vale and Amara Keene as they ask about recovery, life, love, consent, and faithful relationship.

The show

Cast and conversation format

Episodes are built as table conversations: Maya speaks with a warm Midwestern-style voice, Amara speaks with an Australian voice, and Father Rowan answers slowly as a religious priest. Both cohosts speak from recovery, and each episode keeps that history grounded in consent, practical boundaries, and one concrete practice.

Maya Vale

The younger adult cohost, voiced with a warm Midwestern style. Maya is in recovery from compulsive sexual behavior and asks direct questions about honesty, desire, tenderness, and daily practice inside love.

Father Rowan

An older religious priest voiced in a slow British-Boston-inspired cadence. He answers with calm counsel, consent, and spiritual discipline.

Amara Keene

The older of the two adult cohosts, voiced with an Australian style, and Maya's romantic partner. Amara is in recovery from fentanyl addiction and presses for practical answers, boundaries, and grounded care.

The cohosts ask Father Rowan how partners can help each other through difficulty without turning care into control.
