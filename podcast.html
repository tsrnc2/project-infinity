The show

What each episode does

Episodes are designed as short practices: one teaching, one reflection question, one calendar reading, and one concrete act of service or repair.

Calendar Reflection

Read the current house, tone, day sign, moon gate, and year sign as a weekly practice guide.

Founding Texts

Offer short readings from the covenant, the Book of Turning Days, and seasonal liturgies.

Service Stories

Share concrete repairs, lessons learned, ethical failures, and useful practices from circles.

Production Covenant

  • Episodes should not pressure listeners to join, give money, or isolate from outside support.

  • Teachings should end with a concrete practice, not vague inspiration.
  • Corrections and concerns should be invited publicly.
