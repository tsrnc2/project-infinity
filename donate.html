Giving covenant

Transparency and boundaries

Support must remain voluntary, documented, and proportionate. A community built around transformation cannot use money to create fear, rank, secrecy, or dependence.

  • Donations do not buy membership, leadership, influence, forgiveness, or special access.
  • Members should not be pressured to give publicly or beyond their means.
  • Local circles should keep a clear ledger of income, expenses, and service projects.
  • Tax-deductible status should not be claimed unless the proper legal status exists.
  • Financial questions, refusals, and concerns must be welcomed without retaliation.
