How to read the signs

One date, many symbols

A complete date is read as a stack of signs. The will chooses the practice; the symbols name the forces shaping that practice.

1. Count Which cycle is speaking: solar body, sacred pulse, moon mind, or year world.
2. Tone The number-force of the day, from initiating will to transforming completion.
3. Sign The day image that gives the tone a face, tool, virtue, and devotional task.
4. Practice The concrete act by which willpower becomes character and character becomes service.
